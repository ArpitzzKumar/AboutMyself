The Pirate Code

📜

  • Never stop learning. — The sea is vast, and there are always new waters to chart. Stagnation is the true enemy.

  • Write clean code. — A ship that's well-maintained weathers any storm. Messy rigging leads to disaster.

  • Fail forward. — Every shipwreck teaches you how to build a better vessel. Fear of failure is mutiny against progress.

  • Share the treasure. — Knowledge hoarded is treasure buried and forgotten. Teach, mentor, and lift others up.
